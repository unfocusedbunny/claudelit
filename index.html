>

Every day a little robot asks GitHub two things: how many new issues did people open on the Claude Code repo, and how many have a complaint word in the title?

Complaint words: degraded, degradation, degrades, nerfed, dumber, got worse, getting worse, quality regression, low quality, poor quality, output quality.

Counting words is rough (one "degrades" was about auto mode, not the model). Most grumbling never makes it to GitHub. And two lines moving together doesn't mean one caused the other.

The test scores come from LiveNerf. It asks Opus 5.5 the same 78 tricky questions every day through Claude Code, and 12 to Opus 5 as a control. It only calls something a real change if the shift is at least 3 points, holds for two 10-day windows in a row, and doesn't show up in the control too. Their scores start on 24 Sep.

The test panel starts at 0%, so small wiggles look small.

How Claude feels depends on four things: the model, the effort setting, the app version and the task. LiveNerf freezes everything except the model. So if complaints go up and the tests stay flat, it's probably a setting, the app, or vibes. If the tests drop too, the model itself changed.
